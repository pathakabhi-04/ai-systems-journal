# Weekly log

One entry a week. The honest version, including the weeks where nothing worked.

Each file is `week-NN.md`, counted from when I started (week 01 = the week of 2026-10-12), and
follows the template in `week-01.md`. The numbering matches the design sketches: log week 01
covers design week 01.

The field that matters most is the last one. Wasted prompts are the thing that compounds, and
after three months this folder is the raw material for a write-up.
